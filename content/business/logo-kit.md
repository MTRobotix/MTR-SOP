## Email signature

Sized for email: 440 × 108 px, shown at 220 px wide.

[Email signature logo](attachments/logo-email-signature.png)

1. Download the file above.
2. Open your email signature settings (Gmail: **Settings → See all settings → General → Signature**. Outlook: **Settings → Mail → Compose and reply**).
3. Add your name, title, `mtrobotix@gmail.com` and phone on separate lines.
4. Insert the image under them and upload the downloaded file.
5. Set the image width to 220 px if your email app lets you.
6. Send a test email to yourself and check it on a phone.

Expected: the logo shows sharp, about the width of your name line.

Source: `content/business/attachments/logo-email-signature.png`

| Use                     | Value                                       |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Navy — dots, ROBOTIX    | `#1d2d3d`                                   |
| Red — largest dots, MT  | `#e5463d`                                   |

| Background              | White `#ffffff` or navy `#1d2d3d`           |
| Wordmark font           | Barlow Medium (500), letter spacing 0.28 em |

Source: `MTR-Site/src/styles/tokens.css`, logo concept 41.

## Rules

- Keep clear space around the logo at least the height of the "M" in the wordmark.
- Smallest size: primary logo 120 px wide on screen or 30 mm in print; mark alone 24 px.
- Never stretch, rotate, recolour (except the one-colour file), add shadows or outlines.
- Never change which dots are red, or move the wordmark relative to the mark.
- Never place the colour logo on a busy photo. Use the navy version on a solid band instead.

Source: Logo concept 41 "Halftone", October 2026.
