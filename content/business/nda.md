## When to use

Sign the mutual NDA **before** you share anything in this list, or receive anything like it:

- MetriQ source code, AI models, training data, drawings, the BOM, supplier details or prices.
- A demo or pilot on a customer's line — the customer's product images, video and measurements are their confidential information.
- Business plans or financials with an investor or lender.

Not needed for what is already public: the website, the one-pager, published specs.

Source: Template drafted by Claude for MTR, October 2026.

## Fill in and send

1. Download the [Word template](attachments/mtr-mutual-nda.docx).
2. Fill in every highlighted field in **Part A**. Leave Part B (the terms) unchanged.
3. Pick the governing law in Part A item 8: **Ontario** for Canadian counterparties, **Vietnam (VIAC arbitration)** for Vietnamese ones.
4. Leave Part A item 7 as **Not allowed** unless the customer agrees MTR may use their anonymised images to improve MetriQ for everyone.
5. Save as PDF and send it to the counterparty.
6. If they ask to change Part B, send the changes to Thong Huynh before agreeing.
7. Both sides sign — on paper, or with an e-signature tool.

Expected: one PDF signed by both Parties, with every Part A field filled in.

Source: Template drafted by Claude for MTR, October 2026.

## Key terms

| Term                 | What the template says                                                                           |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Type                 | Mutual — both sides protect what the other shares                                                |
| What is protected    | Anything marked confidential or obviously confidential, shared in any form                       |
| Use                  | Only for the Purpose in Part A                                                                   |
| Who may see it       | Staff, contractors, advisers and investors who need it and are bound by confidentiality          |
| Equipment and models | No reverse engineering of hardware, software, AI models or samples                               |
| Production Data      | MTR uses customer images only for that customer; training for others only if allowed, anonymised |
| Return               | Return or destroy within 15 days of a request                                                    |
| Duration             | Disclosures for 2 years; duty lasts 3 more years; trade secrets, code and models — no end        |
| Law                  | Ontario (Toronto courts) or Vietnam (VIAC arbitration), chosen in Part A                         |

- Record each signed NDA: counterparty, date signed, end of Disclosure Period.
