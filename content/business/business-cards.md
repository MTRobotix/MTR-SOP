## Card layout

Every card uses the same two sides. The design file is the MTR business cards canvas: <https://claude.ai/artifact/2XpajimoFnK7mn3CdhjLQn>

| Item       | Value                                                                                              |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Size       | 3.5 × 2 in (88.9 × 50.8 mm), landscape                                                             |
| Background | `color-bg-soft` `#f2f1ec` on both sides                                                            |
| Front      | Primary logo `logo-lockup.svg`, centred, 176 px wide on the 336 px artboard; nothing else          |
| Back       | `logo-mark.svg` cut off at the bottom-left corner; name and title top-right; contacts bottom-right |
| Name       | Barlow Condensed 600, `color-text` `#17181a`                                                       |
| Title      | Barlow 500, `color-text-muted` `#5b5d63`                                                           |
| Contacts   | Barlow 400/500, right-aligned: email, phone, city                                                  |
| Margins    | Text at least 1/4 in (24 px) from the top and right edges, 20 px from the bottom edge              |

Logo files: [Logo kit](/d/business/logo-kit#which-file-to-use).

Source: MTR business cards canvas, artboards "A · Light — front" and "Back 12", chosen by Thong Huynh, November 2026.

## Print files

- **Print PDF**: page 1 front, page 2 back. Page size 3.75 × 2.25 in: the 3.5 × 2 in card plus 1/8 in bleed on every side. Only the background and the logo mark run into the bleed.
- **PNG**: 1050 × 600 px (3.5 × 2 in at 300 dpi), no bleed. For previews, email and chat.
- **Blank template**: [back with placeholders](attachments/business-card-back-template.png) · [print PDF with placeholders](attachments/business-card-print-template.pdf). Shows where each line goes; do not print it.

> [!WARNING]
> The 1/8 in bleed is a common default, not a printer's spec. Check it against the printer's requirements before ordering.

Source: rendered from the MTR business cards canvas, 2026-11-22.

## Make a card for a new person

1. Open the MTR business cards canvas.
2. Duplicate one of the team card backs.
3. Replace the name, title, email and phone. Keep the layout, colours and fonts.
4. Write the email as `Given.Family@mtrobotix.com` and the phone in international format (`+84 …` or `+1 …`).
5. Add the person to the table in [Team card details](#team-card-details).
6. Export the front and the back as PDF and send both to the printer.
7. Add the back PNG and print PDF to `content/business/attachments/` as `business-card-back-<given-name>.png` and `business-card-print-<given-name>.pdf`, and link them in the table.

Expected: the back matches the other team cards, and no text touches the logo mark.
