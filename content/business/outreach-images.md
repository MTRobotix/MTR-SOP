## MetriQ dashboard

The MetriQ Dashboard on a lemon line: raw camera view and MetriQ AI view side by side, the last lemon with its length, width and height, and a summary of the last hour. Same image as on the MetriQ page of the website, at 4320 × 3030 px (PNG, 2.9 MB).

[MetriQ dashboard on a lemon line](attachments/metriq-dashboard-lemon.png)

1. Download the file above.
2. Email or LinkedIn message: attach it under your first paragraph.
3. Slides: place it full width. It stays sharp on a 4K screen.

> [!WARNING]
> The camera views are MetriQ AI output on a lemon line. The numbers on the screen (8,034 products, 97.8% in spec, the sizes) are sample values for the demo screen, not measured results. Do not quote them as results.
