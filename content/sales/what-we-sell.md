## BoltEye

Automated inspection for live production lines. Conveyor with custom optical hardware and real-time vision. Detects surface defects down to ≤ 2 mm with end-to-end latency under 100 ms.

Good fit: a line where people inspect parts by eye for surface defects.

## SensQ

Compact autonomous mobile robot for small and medium manufacturers. Maps the site, navigates with ROS 2 Nav2, and docks itself to charge using AprilTags.

Good fit: repeat material moves inside one building, without buying a full industrial fleet.
