## Stages and gates

Move to the next stage only when its gate is met. Times are internal targets, not customer commitments. Templates for each stage: [Business document set](/d/documents/document-set#which-document-when).

| -- | -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| 0  | Prepare              | Target list, Vietnamese deck, demo video, lemon-line images, NDA ready.                                                      | Named contact, meeting booked                                             | —              |

## Gather requirements

Goal: a requirements table the customer confirms in writing, each line with a target and a test method. It becomes proposal section 2 and the FAT/SAT protocol.

1. After the first intro, send a short message asking for a site visit date, the people who will attend, a few line photos and product samples. Do not send the full form to fill in.
2. At the site visit, ask: "Show us how your operator decides pass or fail, including a borderline part."
3. Fill the requirements form with each owner present. The MTR engineer fills the internal site survey sheet at the same time.
4. Collect samples. Customer QA labels each one.
5. Within 2 business days, send the completed form back for the customer to correct and confirm. Open items keep an owner. Silence is not approval.
6. Any later change goes through a change order.

Source: MetriQ sales playbook, drafted by Claude with Thong Huynh, November 2026.

## Site visit agenda

| Min   | Topic                                                                                                |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0–10  | Problem, cost, sponsor                                                                               |
| 10–30 | Current inspection; good, defective and borderline parts                                             |
| 30–60 | Line walk: width, speed, free height, mounting, power, air, reject point, lighting; photos and video |
| 60–75 | Acceptance targets, monitoring and alerts, samples, data rules                                       |
| 75–90 | Budget, approvals, actions with owners, next date                                                    |

Bring:

- Printed requirements form and internal site survey sheet
- NDA in 2 copies if not signed
- Tape measure, ruler for photos, lux meter or phone app
- Charged phone or camera
- Cold box, ice packs, zip bags and labels for food samples
- Tablet with the MetriQ demo video
- PPE required by the plant

## Acceptance definitions

- **Escape rate** = defective parts accepted ÷ defective parts tested.
- **False-reject rate** = good parts rejected ÷ good parts tested.
- Agree for each metric: defect class, sample size, reference labels by customer QA, line speed, lighting, number of runs, retest rule.
- Zero escapes in a small test does not prove zero escapes in production. State sample sizes in the proposal.

Approved product numbers: [Product one-liners](/d/marketing/product-messaging#approved-numbers). Any other number is a per-project target proven at FAT/SAT.

Source: MetriQ sales playbook, drafted by Claude with Thong Huynh, November 2026.

## Proposal and quote

1. Wait until the customer has confirmed the requirements form and the feasibility test is done.
2. Fill the project proposal and the quotation from the [templates](/d/documents/document-set#which-document-when).
3. Present both in a 30–45 minute meeting, then send the PDFs.
4. Note the validity date: 30 days from the quotation date.

If the customer asks for a price earlier, give an indicative range in writing with its assumptions. Do not send a formal quotation before stage 5.

Source: MetriQ sales playbook, drafted by Claude with Thong Huynh, November 2026.

## Vietnam compliance

No single certificate covers a vision station. Clear each item per project before delivery. Planning checklist, not legal advice.

| Item                 | Action                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Company              | Registered entity issuing VAT e-invoices. Same name on quotation, invoice, label and NDA.                                                                                                |
| Components           | Model-specific conformity documents from each Vietnam supplier, including hợp quy where a QCVN applies. Prefer a PC without Wi-Fi or Bluetooth; otherwise check the ICT conformity list. |
| Electrical           | Licensed electrician checks wiring, earthing, breaker, cable glands. Record the results.                                                                                                 |
| Label                | Vietnamese label: product name, company name and address, serial number, power rating, truthful origin.                                                                                  |
| AI and personal data | Record the AI risk classification. Aim cameras away from people; if people are filmed, apply the Personal Data Protection Law.                                                           |
| Food lines           | Non-contact station. No loose fasteners above product, closed enclosure, cleanable surfaces.                                                                                             |

Sources: [Circular 29/2025](https://congbao.chinhphu.vn/van-ban/thong-tu-so-29-2025-tt-bkhcn-46603/59796.htm), [AI Decree 142/2026](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?docid=218029\&pageid=27160), [Personal Data Protection Law](https://chinhphu.vn/?classid=1\&docid=214590\&orggroupid=1\&pageid=27160), Vietnam launch checklist, November 2026.

## Message templates

First outreach:

```text
Kính gửi anh/chị [Tên],

MTRobotix phát triển MetriQ, trạm camera lắp trên băng tải hiện có, tự phát hiện lỗi và loại sản phẩm hỏng. Lắp đặt trong vài ngày, không dừng dây chuyền.

Đính kèm: video demo 2 phút và màn hình MetriQ trên dây chuyền chanh.

Anh/chị có thể dành 30 phút để chúng tôi xem cách nhà máy đang kiểm tra [sản phẩm] không?

[Tên] · MTRobotix · [điện thoại]
```

After the first meeting, to book the site visit:

```text
Kính gửi anh/chị [Tên],

Cảm ơn anh/chị đã đồng ý triển khai bước tiếp theo với MetriQ. Bên em xin hẹn khảo sát tại nhà máy khoảng 90 phút, dự kiến [ngày 1] hoặc [ngày 2]. Bên em sẽ xem dây chuyền, đo đạc và cùng anh/chị hoàn thiện phiếu khảo sát tại chỗ.

Anh/chị giúp bên em:
- Sắp xếp người phụ trách QA, sản xuất và bảo trì cùng tham gia.
- Gửi trước vài ảnh hoặc video ngắn dây chuyền (nếu tiện).
- Chuẩn bị mẫu sản phẩm: hàng đạt, hàng lỗi và hàng ranh giới.

Nếu cần, hai bên ký NDA trước buổi khảo sát. Sau khảo sát, bên em gửi lại phiếu đã điền trong 2 ngày để anh/chị kiểm tra và xác nhận.

Trân trọng,
[Tên] · MTRobotix · [điện thoại]
```
