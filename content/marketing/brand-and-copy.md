## Names

| Use           | Never                               |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| MTRobotics    | MTR (retired name)                  |
| MTR-Q         | BoltEye, Bolt Eye, Bolteye, BOLTEYE |
| SensQ         | Sensq, SENSQ, Sens-Q                |
| the robot arm | Any product name — it has none yet  |

## Voice

- MTRobotics is an engineer-led studio. Write first-person plural ("we build") or founder voice ("I build").
- Plain words. Short sentences. Numbers with units.
- No hype words: "revolutionary", "cutting-edge", "world-class", "seamless".

## Facts

Every fact about the founder or company must be real and verifiable. The approved list is `~/MTR/site/src/data/site.ts`.

- Never invent headcount, offices, clients, partners, awards or revenue.
- The robot arm is always "in development" / "work in progress".
- Product specs: only the numbers in [Product one-liners](/d/marketing/product-messaging#approved-numbers).

## Visual identity

White, minimal, glass. Off-white ground, one steel-blue accent (`#3d5a80`) used sparingly, pill buttons, frosted cards over photos. No blueprint frames, no `+` marks, no emoji.
